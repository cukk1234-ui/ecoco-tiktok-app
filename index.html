Product-video publishing with review, evidence and creator control.

Ecoco Creator Studio is a TikTok publishing workflow for authorized Ecoco Taiwan creators. It prepares owned short-form product education videos, shows the creator the account and publishing options, sends approved content through TikTok official APIs, and records evidence for daily social reporting.

Not a login-only page

This site explains the product, requested scopes, publishing flow, user controls, data use, review demo and safety boundaries.

Built for authorized creators

The platform is designed for creators and operators who are authorized to publish Ecoco-owned product education videos in Taiwan.

Official API first

The workflow uses TikTok official APIs for account authorization, publishing and read-only reporting. It does not collect passwords, cookies or browser sessions.

Product scope

A publishing system for product education videos.

Ecoco Creator Studio helps prepare and publish short-form videos that show real home-goods use cases: storage, cleaning, kitchen organization, bathroom organization and small-space living. The content is owned or licensed by Ecoco and reviewed before publishing.

The product is not a scraping tool, bot farm or bulk spam publisher. It is a creator-facing workflow with explicit authorization, visible account context and creator-controlled posting settings.

  • Traditional Chinese video content for Taiwan audiences.
  • Human confirmation before a video is sent to TikTok.
  • Commercial content disclosure is part of the publishing checklist.
  • Missing metrics are reported as unavailable, not as zero.

Requested TikTok scopes

Each permission has a narrow purpose.

Scope Purpose inside Ecoco Creator Studio
user.info.basicIdentify the authorized TikTok creator account and display account context before publishing.
user.info.statsRead follower and public account statistics for daily reporting when TikTok provides the data.
video.listVerify published videos and read available performance data for evidence-based reporting.
video.uploadUpload the creator-approved Ecoco video asset to TikTok.
video.publishPublish only after the creator confirms video, caption, privacy, interaction and disclosure settings.

Service region

Ecoco Creator Studio supports Ecoco Taiwan publishing workflows and Traditional Chinese content. It is not offered in mainland China, does not target mainland China users, and does not require access from mainland China IP addresses for review or operation.
