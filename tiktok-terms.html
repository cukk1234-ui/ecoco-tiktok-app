Terms of Service

These terms govern use of Ecoco Creator Studio, a creator-controlled publishing workflow for Ecoco product education videos.

Who may use the product

Ecoco Creator Studio is available to creators, operators and reviewers who are authorized by Ecoco Taiwan to prepare, publish or verify Ecoco-owned or Ecoco-licensed short-form product education videos. Users must comply with these terms, TikTok's terms, TikTok developer policies and applicable laws.

Allowed use

  • Authorize a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit.
  • Select Ecoco-owned or properly licensed video content.
  • Review title, caption, hashtags, privacy level, interaction settings and commercial-content disclosure before publishing.
  • Use TikTok official APIs for upload, publish and read-only reporting when the app has permission.
  • Keep publication evidence and necessary operational logs for audit and duplicate-prevention purposes.

Prohibited use

Users may not upload content they do not have rights to use, impersonate another account, hide commercial content, bypass TikTok login, CAPTCHA, review, warnings or platform restrictions, scrape TikTok, collect TikTok passwords or cookies, or use the product for spam, unlawful, misleading, infringing or unsafe content.

User control and responsibility

Before content is sent to TikTok, the user is responsible for confirming the selected account, video, text, privacy setting, interaction settings and required disclosures. TikTok may restrict, delay, reject, remove or limit content under its own policies. Users may stop using the product or revoke authorization at any time.

Availability and changes

Ecoco Creator Studio is provided as-is. TikTok API availability, scope approvals, platform policies and local device conditions may affect publishing and reporting. Ecoco may update the product or these terms to preserve safety, compliance and compatibility.

Contact

For product questions, authorization removal, data deletion or security concerns, contact Ecoco through Ecoco's official social accounts or official store support channel.
