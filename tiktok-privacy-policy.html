Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Ecoco Creator Studio handles data authorized through TikTok and data created during the publishing workflow.

Data we process

  • TikTok account identifiers, display name, avatar and authorized scopes returned by TikTok Login Kit.
  • Access token, refresh token, token expiry time and authorization status.
  • Creator-selected video, title, caption, hashtags, privacy level, interaction settings and commercial-content disclosure selections.
  • Upload or publish request identifiers, processing status, error codes, timestamps, public URLs or video IDs when provided by TikTok.
  • Available follower, video-list and performance metrics when the authorized TikTok API scopes provide them.

How we use data

Data is used to verify the authorized TikTok account, display the creator's publishing options, upload creator-approved Ecoco video content, check publication status, prevent duplicate publishing, generate evidence-based daily reports and support operational audit. We do not sell TikTok user data, create unrelated advertising profiles, or publish on behalf of accounts that are not authorized for this workflow.

Storage and security

Authorization tokens are stored in the authorized operator's macOS Keychain or an access-controlled local runtime. Publishing queues and operational logs are stored locally for workflow and audit purposes. The product uses OAuth 2.0, PKCE and state validation. It does not collect TikTok passwords, read browser cookies, copy browser sessions, or bypass TikTok login, CAPTCHA, warnings or review controls.

Sharing and retention

We send only creator-approved publishing data and authorized read-only metric requests to TikTok official APIs. We do not disclose TikTok user data to third-party marketing lists or data brokers. Local publishing records are kept only as long as needed for operations, audit, duplicate prevention and legal compliance. Tokens are removed when authorization is revoked or deletion is completed.

Control, revocation and deletion

Authorized users may stop using Ecoco Creator Studio, revoke the app in TikTok's authorization settings, or request deletion of locally stored TikTok authorization and publishing records. After revocation, Ecoco Creator Studio no longer calls TikTok APIs for that account.

Contact

For access, correction, revocation, deletion or security questions, contact Ecoco through Ecoco's official social accounts or official store support channel.
