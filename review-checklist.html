What the reviewer should confirm

  • The public website is fully developed and not only a login page or temporary landing page.
  • The product purpose is clear: creator-controlled publishing and evidence-based reporting for Ecoco product education videos.
  • Requested TikTok scopes are documented with narrow use cases.
  • The workflow includes user confirmation before a video is sent to TikTok.
  • The product states that it does not bypass login, CAPTCHA, warnings, review or platform restrictions.
  • The Terms and Privacy Policy are visible from the homepage without opening a hidden menu.

Suggested review path

  1. Open the homepage and verify product identity, service region and navigation.
  2. Open App review details and inspect end-to-end creator flow.
  3. Open Terms and Privacy Policy from the visible navigation.
  4. Play the representative video to verify the type of content handled.
  5. Use TikTok Login Kit in the review environment with a reviewer or authorized test account.
  6. Confirm the app displays account context and asks the creator to review posting settings.
  7. Confirm publication and metrics reporting are based on TikTok API evidence or explicitly marked unavailable.

Known limitation until approval

Public direct posting is not claimed before TikTok grants the required Content Posting API approval. If that approval is unavailable, the product keeps TikTok jobs in a blocked or native-handoff-ready state and does not mark them as publicly published.
